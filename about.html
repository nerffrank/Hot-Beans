About Hot Beans Web

Practical websites, built responsibly.

Hot Beans Web is a growing development company that creates accessible, responsive and lower-impact websites for clients who need reliable digital services.

Company Profile

We support businesses with recruitment pages, service websites, online forms and content updates. Our team values clear communication, careful testing and maintainable code.

Our Purpose

We help organisations publish websites that are easy to use, quick to load and simple to update after launch.

Our Culture

Trainees work with mentors, receive feedback on real tasks and learn professional habits such as planning, review and documentation.

Our Clients

We work with small businesses, education providers and community organisations that need practical web solutions.

Sustainability Commitments

Our approach to sustainability is simple: reduce waste, measure improvements and avoid adding features that do not help users.

Efficient Assets

Images are compressed and saved in modern formats where possible, helping pages transfer less data.

Lean Code

We prefer semantic HTML, focused CSS and small scripts only when interaction genuinely needs them.

Accessible Design

Clear structure, labels and keyboard-friendly controls improve usability while keeping pages simpler.
